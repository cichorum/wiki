## Botanical Garden with an Intelligent Guide

Our system functions like a newly renovated botanical garden, with knowledge well-organized into interconnected sections that form a coherent landscape. An intelligent guide, intricately familiar with the website, is available to assist in navigating. By conversing with visitors and using relevant, synthesized information, our system operates as a guided exploration rather than a static reading experience.

Each section in the garden corresponds to a node, and trails between these sections correspond to edges in the graph structure. Our AI platform pulls information from a graph of nodes based on the content of the webpages, much like a guide would be familiar with their botanical garden.
